Profile

AI drug discovery leader with a PhD in Biochemistry and an M.S. in Computer Science (AI) from Georgia Tech. I combine more than a decade of structure-based drug discovery with hands-on work in generative protein design, antibody discovery, structure prediction, and model evaluation. I currently serve as Associate Director of Operations at Synbio Technologies, where I work across AIDD and AI-assisted gene synthesis. I am looking for AIDD roles where experimental judgment, computational rigor, and scientific operations meet.
