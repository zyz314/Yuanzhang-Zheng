Profile

Computational structural biologist with a PhD in Biochemistry and experience across academic research and industry drug discovery. My work combines structural biology, protein engineering, and machine learning to design and prioritize antibody and protein candidates. I am particularly interested in the translation from a computational model to a testable molecule: reproducible pipelines, honest uncertainty, and experiments that can falsify a design.
