ESM-2

A protein language model for embeddings, variant effects, and structure-aware representations.

Year
2023
Difficulty
Beginner
Compute

Status
Brief ready

Access note. Good protein embedding baseline

What it does

ESM-2 is a transformer trained with a masked-language-model objective on protein sequences. It produces embeddings that are useful for variant prediction, structure prediction, and downstream classifiers.

It is not a generative design model by itself, although its representations power ESMFold and other tools.

The AI principle

ESM-2 uses a masked language modeling objective: hide some amino acids, predict them from context. The resulting transformer learns evolutionary and structural constraints implicitly from sequence data.

The representations can be used zero-shot (e.g., variant effect scores) or fine-tuned with a small task head. Scaling from 8M to 15B parameters improves many downstream tasks.

What it is good at

  • It is a strong, general protein representation model.
  • It works with small labeled datasets via fine-tuning or probing.
  • It is widely supported and easy to run.

Where it breaks

  • It does not explicitly model 3D structure or ligand context.
  • It can memorize and reproduce biases in sequence databases.
  • Zero-shot variant predictions are not a substitute for experiments.

How I would improve it

  • Add structure-aware or pocket-aware conditioning.
  • Fine-tune on antibody or enzyme-specific data.
  • Calibrate variant-effect predictions across proteins.
