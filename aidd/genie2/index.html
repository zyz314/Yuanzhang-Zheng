Genie2

SE(3)-equivariant diffusion for protein backbone generation and motif scaffolding.

Year
2025
Difficulty

Access note. Check repo for precise publication date

What it does

Genie2 generates protein backbones. It can run unconditionally or scaffold a motif, and it is the direct predecessor of Genie3.

It is a backbone model, so sequence design and validation are separate.

The AI principle

Genie2 uses asymmetric representations: a simple point-cloud noising process in the forward direction and SE(3) frames in the reverse denoising direction. This makes training efficient while keeping the generative process equivariant.

The denoiser uses invariant point attention and pair/single representations, similar to AlphaFold-style architectures. It learns the distribution of protein backbone geometry.

What it is good at

  • It is a clean, efficient SE(3) diffusion model.
  • It performs well on motif scaffolding and unconditional generation.
  • It is a useful baseline for understanding Genie3's improvements.

Where it breaks

  • Backbone-only; no side-chain or ligand chemistry.
  • No sequence generation.
  • Older than Genie3 and less capable on difficult binder tasks.

How I would improve it

  • Move to all-atom side-chain and ligand representations.
  • Add sequence co-generation.
  • Calibrate designability against experimental stability.
