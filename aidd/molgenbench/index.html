MolGenBench

A benchmark for de novo generation and lead optimization in structure-based drug discovery.

Access note. Benchmark rather than generative model

What it does

MolGenBench is not a generative model. It is an evaluation framework that compares molecular generators on de novo design and lead optimization tasks.

It is useful precisely because the field has too many one-number claims. A benchmark can show whether a model is good at generating novel molecules, improving a hit, or satisfying pocket constraints.

The AI principle

There is no single learned architecture to explain. The benchmark defines tasks, splits, metrics, and baselines, then runs generative models through them.

The AI question is evaluation design: which metrics correlate with real drug-discovery success, and which can be gamed by reward hacking or dataset leakage.

What it is good at

  • It makes cross-model comparisons more honest than each paper's own evaluation.
  • It covers both de novo generation and lead optimization, which stress different abilities.
  • It is a good place to start before trusting a new generative model.

Where it breaks

  • A benchmark score is not experimental efficacy.
  • Dataset bias and metric choice can favor models that are good at the benchmark rather than good at design.
  • It does not replace a wet-lab or a prospective docking campaign.

How I would improve it

  • Add prospective, experimentally validated tasks and negative controls.
  • Report synthesizability, selectivity, and ADMET alongside docking and novelty.
  • Encourage submission of failure cases, not only top-scoring molecules.
