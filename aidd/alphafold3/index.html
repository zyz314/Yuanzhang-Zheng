AlphaFold3

The reference all-atom co-folding model for biomolecular complexes.

Year
2024
Difficulty
Advanced
Compute
GPU; high memory
Status
Brief ready

Access note. Public code; weights/access and license restrictions apply

What it does

AlphaFold3 predicts joint structures of proteins, nucleic acids, ligands, and ions. It is the model most other open co-folding systems are compared against.

It is a structure predictor. It does not predict affinity, kinetics, or the effect of a mutation on function.

The AI principle

AF3 replaces much of the Evoformer with a Pairformer trunk and uses a diffusion module to generate atom coordinates. It operates on atoms rather than residues alone, which is why it can represent ligands and modified residues.

The model learns from the PDB and related structural data. Its confidence heads (pLDDT, PAE, pTM) are trained to estimate structural accuracy, not biological activity.

What it is good at

  • It is the strongest general reference for complex structure prediction.
  • It handles a wide range of ligands, ions, and nucleic acids.
  • It is an excellent oracle for filtering designed complexes, with the usual caveats.

Where it breaks

  • Weights are non-commercial and access is controlled; it is not an open-source model in the usual sense.
  • It is static and does not capture conformational ensembles or binding entropy.
  • It can be confidently wrong, especially on novel chemistry and low-homology targets.

How I would improve it

  • Release permissive weights and a reproducible training recipe.
  • Add ensemble and affinity/kinetics heads with proper calibration.
  • Evaluate on prospective, experimentally measured complexes rather than only PDB re-runs.
